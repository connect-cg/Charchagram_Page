Who are we

Charchagram is a civic-tech initiative working to strengthen accountability and transparency in public systems. We build tools and investigate public technology infrastructure and data to make governance more accessible, transparent, and accountable to the citizens.

Our work spans electoral literacy, digital investigations, and public archiving. Our current initiative, RTIgram, aims to make the Right to Information more accessible by simplifying the process of drafting and filing RTI applications, along with creating an open database of public RTIs.

Our initiatives

RTIgram

RTIgram puts the Right to Information Act to work for every citizen. We help people write and file RTI applications that actually get answered — and build a public, searchable record of the ones already filed. Most RTI responses are read once and forgotten; RTIgram keeps them, so the answer one citizen fought for is there the second time someone else needs it, not just the first.

Electoral Literacy Portal

A collective discussion forum for voters to review candidates’ assets, criminal cases and parliamentary record, and share their views, supported by real-time analysis, dashboards, sentiment tracking, polls and ratings that turn scattered voices into meaningful, actionable dialogue.

Visit portal ↗

Collective Archive

A crowdsourced digital archive where people share written or voice accounts of the 20 July 2026 protest in New Delhi, preserving the many ways it was experienced.
